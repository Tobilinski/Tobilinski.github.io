Me

Hi, I'm Tobi — a game developer with a Bachelor of Arts in Game Design and Production. I currently work at Bizar Reality, building immersive VR and AR experiences that push the boundaries of real-time 3D.

I'm comfortable in collaborative environments: contributing to shared codebases, shipping on schedule, and communicating clearly with team members. My Unity work spans UI systems, 3D development, XR (VR/AR) to even custom input programming like Modbus or sim racing gear. I'm always picking up something new!!

I speak German natively, English fluently, and have a working understanding of Afrikaans. Outside of work life, I run YouTube channels documenting motorcycle adventures and outdoor trips as well as mess around in CAD programs.
