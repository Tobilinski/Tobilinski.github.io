Me

I'm comfortable in collaborative environments: contributing to shared codebases, shipping on schedule, and communicating clearly across teams. My Unity work spans UI systems, 3D development, and XR (VR/AR) — and I'm always picking up something new.

I speak German natively, English fluently, and have a working understanding of Afrikaans. Outside the studio, I run YouTube channels documenting motorcycle adventures and outdoor trips.
